## Purpose

This document defines how AI-assisted workflows are integrated into project development and how team members should collaborate with AI tools.

## Team Roles

- **AI Engineer**: Oversees AI-assisted generation, review, and integration.
- **Software Engineer**: Validates AI output and implements business logic.
- **Technical Writer**: Captures AI-derived decisions in documentation.

## AI Collaboration

- Use AI to generate templates, documentation, and scaffolding.
- Validate AI output manually before applying it to the project.
- Track changes made through AI with clear revision notes.

## Prompting Expectations

- Provide concise and explicit instructions.
- Avoid ambiguous or open-ended prompts.
- Include success criteria and constraints in prompts.

## Documentation and Traceability

- Record AI-generated design decisions in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
- Reference AI workflow policies in [PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md).
